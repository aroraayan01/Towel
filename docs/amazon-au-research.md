# Amazon Australia: what sells (checked 24 September 2026)

Taken from the amazon.com.au Best Sellers lists and search results for the categories xomexo sells. Amazon is the mass market; xomexo is priced above it on purpose, so treat these figures as the floor, not the target.

## Bedding

- The top-selling bedding is **cheap microfibre**: quilt cover and sheet sets at $35 to $40 from Wake In Cloud and Amazon Basics. Amazon Basics sheets have 437,000 reviews. Don't compete there.
- **Mulberry silk pillowcases** are #2 in all of Bedding & Linen (a 2-pack at about $20). There's clear demand, and a premium 22-momme version is easy to justify.
- **Quilts are sold by weight and size.**
  - Weights run from 150 to 800gsm, and listings call them summer, all-season or winter.
  - Australian sizes are Single, Double, Queen 210×210, King 240×210 and Super King 270×240.
  - "Doona" is a common search word.
- **Australian merino wool quilts** make the top 15 at $119+ (one listing has 756 reviews). This is the best premium opening, which is why it's the lead bedding product.
- Bamboo and "cooling" quilts sell well for hot nights.
- **Throws** are almost all fleece at $30 to $60, in the standard 127×152cm size. Knitted, waffle-cotton, muslin and faux-fur throws also rank.

## Bath

- Bath towels are a pack-and-price market: 4-packs of 450 to 550gsm cotton at $40 to $60.
- Premium signals that rank: 600gsm Egyptian cotton sets, bath sheets (90×165cm) and zero-twist cotton.
- Turkish towels and sand-free beach towels rank well, including Dock & Bay at around 8,700 reviews.
- Microfibre hair towels and exfoliating cloths are high-volume add-ons. Not stocked yet, but they'd be cheap basket-builders.

## Rugs

- **Machine-washable** is the most common selling point on best-selling area rugs and runners.
- Popular sizes: runners 80×300cm, area rugs 160×230 and 200×300cm.
- **Rug underlays and anti-slip grippers** rank highly. Worth adding as an add-on at checkout.
- Bath mats: microfibre and "quick dry" diatomite-style mats dominate.

## Leather

| Product | Typical Amazon AU price | xomexo price |
|---|---|---|
| Toiletry (dopp) bag | $65 to $87 | $129 |
| Weekender / duffel | $105 to $135 (premium brands up to $290) | $449 |
| Card holder | $48 to $140 (Secrid about $114) | $69 |
| Valet / catchall tray | $44 to $117 (Italian handmade at the top) | $89 |
| Pouf / ottoman | mostly **faux** leather, $120 to $150 | $549, genuine leather |

Real leather is the differentiator: most Amazon poufs and trays are PU (fake leather). Personalisation (initials) is widely offered on Amazon toiletry bags and wallets, so it's expected rather than special. That's why initials are offered on every leather bag and small leather good.
